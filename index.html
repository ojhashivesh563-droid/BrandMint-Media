How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages, features and whether you need e-commerce. After a short call we send a clear, fixed quote, with no surprises later.

What's the minimum ad budget to work with you?

It depends on your market and goals. We work with brands at different stages. Book a call and we'll be honest about what makes sense for your situation, even if that means starting small.

How long before we see results?

Performance campaigns usually show early signals in 2 to 3 weeks, with meaningful results in 60 to 90 days as campaigns learn and optimise. A new website or landing page can start converting from launch day. We set honest expectations from day one.

Do I need a new website to run ads?

Not always. If your current site converts well, we'll use it. If it's leaking leads or sales, we'll recommend a focused landing page first, because sending paid traffic to a weak page wastes budget.

Which ad platforms do you manage?

Meta (Facebook & Instagram), Google (Search, Performance Max, YouTube, Display), LinkedIn and marketplace ads. We recommend platforms based on where your customers actually are.

What industries do you work with?

Startups, D2C brands, real estate, skincare, EdTech, lifestyle, F&B, interiors and personal brands. If you're a modern business looking to grow digitally, we're likely a good fit.

What makes BrandMint Media different?

We build growth systems, not just campaigns. One team handles your website, ads and tracking, so there's no blame game, and we think strategically before we spend a single rupee of yours.
